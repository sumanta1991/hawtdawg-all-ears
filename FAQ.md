# FAQ

## How many photos can I post?

We know you want to show off your fabulous furry face, so we have given you space to upload upto 15 photos.
For those who are camera shy, we recomended posting at least one to bring your profile some attention.
Showcase your best self whether that means a fresh from the groomer gloumour shot or an action shot from your last game of fetch.

## Where do I list my favourite treats?

Open the hawtdawg app and click on "edit profile."
